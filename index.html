About Me

I am a third-year Computer Engineering student at the University of Florida with primary interests in embedded systems and software development. I enjoy problem-solving through programming and am especially interested in how software interacts with the hardware beneath it. That intersection drew me to computer engineering, as it lets me approach problems from both software and hardware perspectives.

My coursework has given me experience across both the software and hardware aspects of computer engineering, through courses such as Microprocessor Applications, Operating Systems, Data Structures and Algorithms, Digital Logic, and Digital Design. Through these courses, labs, and projects, I've worked with languages such as C, C++, and assembly, used Git and GitHub for version control and collaboration, and gained hands-on experience with microcontrollers, digital hardware, and FPGA development. These experiences have strengthened my understanding of how higher-level software depends on lower-level system architecture and hardware.

Outside the classroom, I apply programming to ideas that genuinely interest me. One project I am currently developing is HiLo, a web application designed to teach programming concepts progressively through pseudocode, C, and assembly. Developing this project has allowed me to combine web application development with my interest in low-level computing and explore how the same problems can be understood at multiple levels of abstraction.

I am currently seeking Summer 2027 internships in software engineering and embedded software development. I aim to apply my computer engineering background to larger-scale projects while contributing the technical skills I have developed through coursework and personal projects. Ultimately, I want an internship to be more than another line on my resume; I want to leave knowing that I contributed to meaningful work, learned from the people working alongside me, and became a stronger engineer as a result.
